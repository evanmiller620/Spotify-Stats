                                The file you load is unzipped and processed in your browser. The result is saved in this browser only; Remove my data deletes it.

                                What can leave your device, and when

                                • Friends. Opening Friends with your own data loaded uploads a summary (the songs, artists, play counts and listening times behind the charts, not your raw files) so people you give your link to can see it. Stop sharing removes it and the link stops working.
                                • Playing a song. Songs play in Spotify's embedded player, which loads from Spotify. If it can't, the guessing game looks up a 30-second preview in Apple's iTunes catalogue by sending the song's title and artist.
                                • Signing in with Spotify (optional, to save a playlist or pick playlists for the guessing game). The sign-in is kept in this browser and used only for those two things.
